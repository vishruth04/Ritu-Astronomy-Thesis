## 1. Which models for this thesis

| Model | Scale | Purpose | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site / massing | 1:500 or 1:200 | Slope, KSO, Rose Garden, road; sunk dome | Greyboard contours, foam-board blocks |
| **Sectional "light-sequence" model** (the hero model) | 1:100 or 1:50 | Cut through the whole route from Sun Gallery to dome; lit inside with warm-to-cool LEDs fading to darkness | Mount board / MDF shell, acrylic for glass, black card interior, LED strips, fibre optics for stars |
| Detail room model | 1:20 | One key room (Spectrum Chamber or star chamber) with furniture and exhibits | Basswood, acrylic, dichroic film, mirror card |
| Exhibit prototype (optional) | 1:5 or 1:1 | e.g. tactile Moon or orrery table | 3D print, laser-cut ply |

Scale rule: model length = real length / scale (at 1:100, 8,000 mm → 80 mm). Detail must match scale: 1:100 shows form, 1:20 shows furniture.

## 2. Materials and glue

- Foam board 5 mm for massing; mount board (~1.4 mm) for presentation; greyboard for bases and contours; basswood/balsa for structure; MDF or ply bases; acrylic and acetate for glazing.
- PVA for foam, board and basswood (solvent glues melt foam; superglue fogs acrylic; too much PVA warps card). Double-sided tape for reversible work.
- Sustainability: acrylic rated low, foam board medium, balsa good (MSA guide); prefer card, timber and reusable bases.

## 3. Tools

Snap-off or #11 scalpel with spare blades, steel rule, self-healing mat, set squares, fine saw, files, tweezers. Change blades when paper tears. Cut away from the body in several light passes.

## 4. Lighting the model (key for Chiaroscuro)

- 5 V or 12 V LED strips (warm white 2,700 K for the Sun end, cool white/blue toward the dark end), a small dimmer per zone, battery pack or USB.
- Fibre-optic star kit or pin-pricks in black card lit from behind for the star chamber.
- Paint interior dark zones matte black; leave the Sun Gallery white/natural.
- A removable roof or cut section so the jury can see inside; photograph in a dark room with a long exposure.

## 5. Laser cutting and 3D printing

- Draw clean vector cut files (DXF from AutoCAD; separate layers for cut and engrave; check units and kerf). Never laser-cut PVC.
- Test-cut on scrap first; book the laser 7-10 days before a jury.
- Hand-cut early study models; laser-cut once the design is fixed.
- 3D print complex parts (dome shell, tactile Moon, orrery).

## 6. Budget (unverified guide figures)

Starter tool kit ₹4,000-6,000; foam board 5 mm ₹250-450 per A2; mount board ₹120-250 per A2; basswood packs ₹400-1,500. Buy in Chennai art and model shops or online; laser cutting is charged per minute or per sheet.

## 7. Timeline

- Study models: during concept stage (quick, rough)
- Site model: before site/concept review
- Sectional light model: start at design development, finish for pre-final
- Detail model: final jury
