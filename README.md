# Ritu astronomy thesis

Knowledge base for **Rithikaa P.B.'s** B.Des Interior Design capstone (S102BPROJ, Semester 8, Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology, 2023-2027).

**Project:** Astronomy Interactive Museum, Kodaikanal, beside the Kodaikanal Solar Observatory and the Rose Garden.
**Concept:** Chiaroscuro. Visitors move from bright, sunlit galleries about the Sun, through dimmer filtered spaces for the Moon and planets, into a dark chamber for stars and deep space; contrast itself guides the experience.
**Focus:** interiors first (~60%), services and innovation (~25%), site (~15%). A few lightly interactive exhibits; not tech-heavy.

## Key findings so far

1. The site neighbour, KSO (founded 1899), has 125 years of daily solar records and discovered the Evershed effect; its museum is mostly posters. The new museum fills that gap and can tell KSO's story hands-on.
2. Kodaikanal is cool and very humid (87-89% Sep-Nov, ~1,580 mm rain), with a reported 10 m / three-floor height cap: sink the dark zones and dome into the slope, and choose moisture-resistant materials.
3. Eyes need minutes (up to an hour) to adapt to darkness, so the route must step down in stages through "light locks"; this turns the concept into a measurable design rule.
4. A 10-12 m flat, concentric dome with 60-90 seats suits the town and doubles as the event space; Chennai's Birla Planetarium (15 m, 10° tilt) is the nearest live case.
5. Museums are NBC Group D-3 assembly; false ceilings must be non-combustible; dark rooms need step lights, lit exits and alarm-linked house lights.
6. The aquarium projects on the topic sheet (Under, Blue Planet) should be replaced with astronomy cases: Shanghai Astronomy Museum, Rose Center, Peter Harrison Planetarium, Birla Chennai, Pilikula Mangaluru, Turrell skyspaces.
