# Glossary

Status: Draft, 27 Nov 2026. Short definitions of terms used across the research.

## Astronomy

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Astronomical unit (AU) | The average distance from Earth to the Sun, about 150 million km |
| Coelostat | A pair of mirrors driven to follow the Sun, sending its light into a fixed telescope or onto a screen |
| Heliostat | A single mirror that tracks the Sun and reflects sunlight to a fixed point; can bring daylight deep into a building |
| Evershed effect | Outward flow of gas in the penumbra of a sunspot, discovered at Kodaikanal in 1909 |
| Fraunhofer lines | Dark lines in the Sun's spectrum, each caused by an element absorbing its own colours |
| Light-year | The distance light travels in one year, about 9.46 trillion km |
| Nakshatra | One of the 27 lunar mansions of Indian astronomy, star groups along the Moon's path |
| Orrery | A mechanical model of the solar system that shows planets moving round the Sun |
| Penumbra / umbra | The lighter outer ring and the dark core of a sunspot (or of a shadow) |
| Spectroscope | An instrument that splits light into its spectrum to show which elements are present |
| Sunspot | A cooler, darker region on the Sun caused by strong magnetic fields |
| Terminator | The line between the lit and dark halves of the Moon or a planet; craters show best along it |
| Zero Shadow Day | A day when the noon Sun is exactly overhead, so upright objects cast no shadow (twice a year between the tropics) |

## Light and vision

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Chiaroscuro | Strong contrast between light and dark; the project concept |
| Dark adaptation | The eye's slow increase in sensitivity in darkness; minutes for a useful level, up to about an hour for full adaptation |
| Lux (lx) | Unit of light falling on a surface. Office about 300-500 lx; museum objects 50-200 lx; full moonlight under 1 lx |
| Colour temperature (CCT, K) | How warm or cool white light looks; 2,700-3,000 K is warm, 4,000 K neutral, 5,000 K+ cool |
| CRI | Colour rendering index, how faithfully a light shows colours (100 is perfect; 90+ for exhibits) |
| Glare | Discomfort or loss of vision from bright sources in the field of view |
| Light lock | A passage with turns, baffles or double doors that stops light passing between bright and dark rooms |
| Light ladder | This project's sequence of target light levels from the Sun Gallery to the star chamber |
| Raking light | Light at a low angle across a surface, which shows texture and relief through shadow |
| Dichroic glass | Glass with thin metal-oxide layers that transmits one colour and reflects another, changing with the viewing angle |
| Fibre-optic star field | A ceiling with thousands of optical fibres fed by a hidden light source, creating points of light like stars |
| Dark-sky lighting | Outdoor lighting that is shielded, downward, warm and timed to protect the night sky |

## Planetarium and acoustics

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Concentric (flat) dome | Seats arranged in rings facing the centre under a level dome; flexible, good for talks |
| Unidirectional (tilted) dome | Dome tilted with seats facing one way, like a cinema; more immersive, costs more |
| Springline | The level where the dome's curve starts |
| Cove | The ring around the dome's base that hides lights and equipment |
| Perforated dome | A metal dome screen full of tiny holes that lets sound pass through to absorbers behind it |
| Reverberation time (RT) | Time for sound to fade by 60 dB; Sabine formula RT = 0.161 V / A |
| Sabins | Units of sound absorption (m² of perfect absorber) |

## Codes and drawings

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NBC 2016 | National Building Code of India; Part 4 covers fire and life safety |
| Group D (D-2, D-3) | NBC assembly occupancies; museums are D-3 |
| Harmonised Guidelines | The Government of India guidelines for a barrier-free built environment (2015 edition read for this research; updated 2021) |
| RCP | Reflected ceiling plan: the ceiling drawn as seen from below in a mirror, with lights and services |
| BOQ | Bill of quantities, a list of items, quantities and rates for costing |
| BWP ply | Boiling-water-proof plywood (IS 710 marine grade), for humid conditions |
| HDHMR | High-density high-moisture-resistance board, an alternative to MDF in humid places |
| Light-gradient plan | A plan coloured from white to black to show the brightness of each zone |
| SWOT | Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats; a site and project analysis tool |
