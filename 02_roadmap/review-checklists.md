# Review-by-review checklists

Status: Draft, 27 Nov 2026. A tick-list for each review. Review names follow the usual sequence in Indian design colleges; rename them to match the college schedule when it arrives. Each list says what to bring, what reviewers will probably ask, and where the research is.

## Review 1: Literature study and case studies

**Bring**

- [ ] 3 international cases (Shanghai Astronomy Museum, Rose Center, Peter Harrison Planetarium)
- [ ] 2-3 national cases (Birla Planetarium Chennai, Pilikula Mangaluru, optional Nehru Planetarium or Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium Bengaluru)
- [ ] 1-2 live cases with your own photos, sketches and measurements (Birla Chennai is easiest; KSO museum if the trip is possible)
- [ ] Special references: Turrell skyspaces, Newgrange (light as experience)
- [ ] For each case: zoning, circulation, light, materials, exhibits, services, accessibility, inferences (use the case-study template)
- [ ] A comparison table across all cases (size, dome size, seats, light strategy, interactivity, what you take away)
- [ ] Literature: lighting and dark adaptation, planetarium design basics, museum standards
- [ ] A summary of standards: NBC Part 4 classification, Harmonised Guidelines accessibility numbers

**Likely questions**

- Why these cases and not aquariums? (Answer: the aquarium examples were from the ocean option; these are all astronomy and light.)
- What did you learn about light from each case?
- How big should your dome be, and why?

## Review 2: Site selection and analysis

**Bring**

- [ ] Location maps: India → Tamil Nadu → Dindigul district → Kodaikanal → site
- [ ] Site plan with KSO, Rose Garden, roads, approach (SH-156)
- [ ] Contours and slope (ask the guide or the college for the survey; otherwise from Google Earth/topographic sheet, marked as approximate)
- [ ] Climate charts: temperature, rainfall, humidity, sunshine
- [ ] Sun path at 10.2° N: solstice and equinox angles, Zero Shadow Days
- [ ] Wind, views, vegetation, noise, light pollution
- [ ] Bylaws: TN Hill Station Building Rules 1993, 10 m / three-floor height cap (verify), lake and Gymkhana prohibited zones
- [ ] Site photos (if visiting) and a visitor-flow diagram from KSO and the Rose Garden
- [ ] SWOT
- [ ] A "sky and science trail" idea linking KSO, the museum and the Rose Garden

**Likely questions**

- Who owns the land, and is the site buildable?
- How do you meet the height cap with a dome? (Sink the dome and dark rooms into the slope.)
- How will visitors reach it from the bus stand and the lake?

## Review 3: Programme, zoning and concept

**Bring**

- [ ] User study: tourists, school groups, students/researchers, staff
- [ ] Area statement table (≈2,500 m² net, ≈3,100 m² gross), updated to your plan
- [ ] Bubble/adjacency diagram
- [ ] Light-gradient zoning plan (white to black)
- [ ] Visitor-journey storyboard, 8-10 frames
- [ ] Concept sheet: Chiaroscuro, the light ladder with lux values
- [ ] Massing model or 3D blocks showing the dark rooms sunk into the slope
- [ ] Group-rotation timetable for a 40-student visit

**Likely questions**

- What is the measurable design rule behind "Chiaroscuro"? (The light ladder: lux steps and light locks, timed for eye adaptation.)
- How long does a visitor take for the whole route?
- Where do people rest or leave midway?

## Review 4: Material selection

**Bring**

- [ ] Physical material board: real samples (stone, timber/veneer, felt/PET panel, perforated metal, dichroic or coloured glass sample, fabric)
- [ ] Digital board per zone (Sun warm/bright → Moon silver → Stars black/soft)
- [ ] Finishes schedule: floor, wall, ceiling, furniture per room, with fire and moisture ratings
- [ ] Light tests: photograph each sample under bright, dim and raking light
- [ ] Moisture strategy: IS 710 BWP/marine ply, HDHMR, sealed finishes for 87-89% humidity
- [ ] Fire strategy: non-combustible ceilings, fire-retardant fabrics

**Likely questions**

- How do these materials behave in Kodaikanal's humidity?
- Why black in the dark rooms; will it feel oppressive? (Soft, textured, matt blacks with warm points of light; benches and edges lit.)
- What does it cost? (Have a rough BOQ extract.)

## Review 5: Design development (plans, sections, 3D)

**Bring**

- [ ] Plans 1:100 and key rooms at 1:50 with furniture and exhibits
- [ ] Sections through the full light sequence (Sun → Stars) with the light levels drawn as a tone bar
- [ ] Reflected ceiling plans with lighting layout
- [ ] Interior elevations of each main space
- [ ] 3D views: one hero render per zone, at the right brightness (dark renders must still read on paper)
- [ ] Updated area statement

**Likely questions**

- Walk us from entry to exit. What do I see, hear and feel in each room?
- How is the dome reached and left in the dark?
- Show the wheelchair route.

## Review 6: Services and innovation

**Bring**

- [ ] Lighting layout, dimming zones, emergency lighting and exit plan
- [ ] HVAC zoning with fresh air to the dark rooms and the plant location
- [ ] Dome acoustic section with the Sabine RT calculation
- [ ] Fire safety: exits, travel paths, alarm-linked house lights, non-combustible ceilings
- [ ] Accessibility: route plan, toilets, wheelchair seats, tactile paths, Braille labels
- [ ] Dark-sky exterior lighting plan
- [ ] Group operations diagram (timed entry, rotation)

**Likely questions**

- What happens in a fire during a dome show?
- How do you stop sound and light leaking between rooms?
- What is innovative here? (A building designed around darkness and the eye's adaptation, made safe; and protecting the observatory's sky.)

## Review 7: Detailing

**Bring**

- [ ] 5-7 details at 1:10 / 1:5 (see the room-by-room guide's list)
- [ ] Joinery details: exhibit plinths, counters, benches, with materials and fixings
- [ ] Lighting details: star ceiling access void, cove, step lights, handrail light
- [ ] A BOQ extract for one room

## Review 8: Pre-final

- [ ] All sheets in draft (12-16 A1)
- [ ] Physical model in progress (site model 1:500 + interior sectional model 1:50)
- [ ] Report draft
- [ ] PPT draft and a timed 10-minute talk
- [ ] Walkthrough video storyboard

## Review 9: Final jury and viva

- [ ] Final sheets printed, checked for spelling and scale bars
- [ ] Physical models with working lights (the light ladder lit in the model is the showpiece)
- [ ] Report printed and bound
- [ ] PPT and a 2-3 minute walkthrough video
- [ ] Practise the viva questions (see the viva questions topic)

## After every review

1. Write every comment into the review log the same day.
2. Turn comments into tasks with dates.
3. Update the research file the comment affects.
