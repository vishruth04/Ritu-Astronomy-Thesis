## Per-review routine

1. Read the relevant research file; list the sheets needed.
2. Collect data and sources (add to `sources/SOURCES.md`).
3. Draw (AutoCAD/SketchUp), render, compose sheets.
4. Rehearse a 10-minute talk: problem → concept → how the space resolves it.
5. After the review: write the reviewers' comments into `02_roadmap/review-log.md` and turn them into tasks.

## Suggested weekly rhythm (once the schedule is known)

- Mon-Tue: research and drawings
- Wed-Thu: 3D and renders
- Fri: sheet composition
- Sat: model-making
- Sun: rest / catch-up
