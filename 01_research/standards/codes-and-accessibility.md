## 1. What the museum is, under NBC 2016

From [NBC 2016 Part 4, Fire and Life Safety](https://nmcnagpur.gov.in/assets/300/2025/03/mediafiles/NBC2016-Part-IV.pdf) (A):
- Cl. 3.1.5 Group D, Assembly (50 or more people):
  - **D-3:** no permanent stage, 300 or more persons; explicitly includes **museums, art galleries**. This is the main museum.
  - **D-2:** stage and fixed seats up to 1,000. A dome theatre with fixed seats falls here (or D-4 if under 300 without permanent seating; confirm).
  - D-4: no permanent stage, under 300, no permanent seating.
- Cl. 3.1.3 Group B, Educational: a workshop with 20+ students is educational use; usually treated as part of the main occupancy.
- Cl. 3.1.11: in mixed occupancy, separate with 240-minute fire-rated construction where required; **exit capacity must not decrease in the direction of travel.**
- Cl. 3.3.4: **false ceilings must be non-combustible.** Important for the star ceilings: use gypsum, mineral fibre, metal or fire-rated boards; fibres and fabric must be fire-rated.
- Table 1: exit enclosures and shafts 120 min fire resistance.
- Cl. 3.4.5.6(a) (summary): 2,100 mm clear headroom in exit access.
- Cl. 3.4.6.2 (summary): exit signs, emergency lighting and fire alarm on emergency power.

**Not yet confirmed from text (look up in NBC Part 4 cl. 4):** occupant load factors, maximum travel distance, unit exit width and persons per unit, door and corridor minimums, aisle and row rules. Commonly cited values (K, verify before use): assembly without fixed seats about 0.6-1.4 m² per person; travel distance about 30 m for assembly; exit unit width 500 mm. Do not put these on a sheet until checked.

## 2. Dark-space safety (the critical issue for this concept)

Dark rooms are allowed, but must still be safe:
- Exit signs must stay lit; use low-glare photoluminescent or dimmed LED signs designed into the dark palette.
- Emergency lighting that switches on automatically on power failure or alarm.
- Step and aisle lighting in the dome theatre and dark chamber (low-level, shielded).
- Fire alarm that also **raises house lights** in dark rooms.
- No steps in dark zones where avoidable: use ramps at gentle slopes, or mark edges with light.
- Light locks (two sets of doors or a baffled corridor) must not block exits: design exit routes separately from show entries.

**Museum-specific accessibility (K)** — strong for this thesis because the concept is visual:
- Tactile exhibits for blind and low-vision visitors (raised Moon surface, tactile star maps, Braille labels). Her programme already lists "tactile interactive exhibits"; make this a headline.
- Exhibit tops at 750-900 mm with knee space so wheelchair users and children can use them.
- Audio descriptions; induction loop in the dome theatre.
- In dark zones: continuous handrail as a guide line, tactile floor change at thresholds, no protruding objects.
- Seats for rest along the route (older visitors adapt slowly to darkness; see lighting file).

## 5. On the sheets

One compliance sheet is enough at most reviews: occupancy classification, exits plan with travel paths, accessible route diagram, emergency lighting strategy for dark zones, and a table of standards used.
