## 2. Notes per case

### Shanghai Astronomy Museum, Lingang (Ennead Architects, opened 18 July 2021)
Sources: [Ennead](https://www.ennead.com/work/shanghai-astronomy-museum), [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2021/08/09/shanghai-astronomy-museum-tennead-architects-designs/amp/), [Architect Magazine Q&A](https://www.architectmagazine.com/design/q-a-enneads-thomas-wong-on-the-shanghai-astronomy-museum_o/) (B)
- About 39,000 m² (420,000 sq ft); called the largest museum dedicated solely to astronomy. Exhibit design: Xenario.
- Three "instruments": **Oculus** (a circle of sunlight tracks across the plaza and lands on a circular platform at summer-solstice noon), **Inverted Dome** (glass roof over the atrium, end of the route, open view of the sky), **Sphere** (planetarium, appears to float).
- Circulation: a 720° spiral ramp traces "orbital" movement; compression under a ~40 m cantilever, then release into a light-filled space.
- Mostly white interior to heighten perceived light; concrete, GFRC, anodised aluminium.
- **Lesson for Ritu:** the building itself tells time and season with light. Her Sun Gallery can do the same at a small scale (oculus + floor calendar).

### Rose Center for Earth and Space, New York (Polshek Partnership; exhibits Ralph Appelbaum, 2000)
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayden_Planetarium) (B)
- 27 m Hayden Sphere inside a six-storey glass cube; upper half Star Theater (fulldome + Zeiss), lower half Big Bang Theater.
- Heilbrunn Cosmic Pathway: a spiral walkway around the sphere with a timeline from the Big Bang; one of the most popular exhibits.
- "Scales of the Universe": objects compared at relative scale against the sphere.
- **Lesson:** her "cosmic scale corridor" has a famous precedent; a ramp that is also a timeline is a strong interior move.

### Peter Harrison Planetarium, Royal Observatory Greenwich (2007)
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Harrison_Planetarium) (B)
- 120 seats in a bronze-clad truncated cone; one side tilted at 51.5° (London's latitude), top parallel to the celestial equator; 50 m from the prime meridian.
- Part of a £17.7m redevelopment of a historic observatory.
- **Lesson:** a historic observatory plus a new, small, symbolic building. Very close to Kodaikanal's situation. Her dome could be tilted at **10.2°** (Kodaikanal's latitude) as a symbolic gesture.

### B.M. Birla Planetarium, Chennai (1988; renovated 2018-19)
Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birla_Planetarium,_Chennai), [fddb](https://www.fddb.org/domes/periyar-science-and-technology-centre/), [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/news/chennai/birla-planetarium-gets-a-makeover) (B)
- In the Periyar Science and Technology Centre campus, Kotturpuram. 15 m perforated aluminium dome, 10° tilt, 223-250 pushback seats (sources differ), Zeiss hybrid projection, 5K.
- Old optical projector kept on display as "The Resting Giant"; Hall of Fame gallery around the theatre.
- **Live-case checklist:** measure entry light lock, seat spacing, aisle lights, console position, pre-show space, exhibit heights; note school-group handling and crowding; photograph (with permission).

### Swami Vivekananda Planetarium, Pilikula, Mangaluru (March 2018)
Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swami_Vivekananda_Planetarium), [Megastar](https://www.megastar.jp/en/swami-vivekananda-planetarium-india/) (B)
- 18 m tilted dome, 170 seats (tilt 15°), Megastar-IIA optical + Digistar 3D 8K; 8 shows a day, 25 min each.
- **Lesson:** show length and frequency for sizing queues and the pre-show space.

### James Turrell skyspaces
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Turrell) (B)
- Enclosed rooms for about 15 people, benches around the edge, an opening in the roof that frames the sky; light at dusk/dawn makes the sky look like a flat canvas. "The Color Inside" (UT Austin, 2013) is one.
- **Lesson:** an event-space terrace or a small "sky room" at the end of the route that frames the real Kodaikanal sky; very low-tech, very strong.

### Newgrange and Louvre Abu Dhabi (K, verify)
- Newgrange (c. 3200 BC): a roof-box above the entrance lets the rising sun at the winter solstice light the inner chamber for about 17 minutes. Ancient building as astronomical instrument; good for the opening sheet.
- Louvre Abu Dhabi (Jean Nouvel, 2017): a 180 m perforated dome of layered patterns creates a "rain of light" of moving sun spots on the floor. Pattern-perforated screens are a buildable way to get Chiaroscuro in her Sun Gallery or cafe.

### Adler Planetarium, Atwood Sphere (1913)
Source: [Adler](https://www.adlerplanetarium.org/?p=309963) (B)
- About 5 m galvanised-steel sphere with 692 holes; visitors sit inside while it rotates and daylight/lamps show stars through the holes. Oldest operating sky simulator.
- **Lesson:** a cheap, physical, beautiful star room. Could inspire her star chamber or a model.
