## 2. Typical Indian interior design thesis structure

From [StudioMatrx, interior design thesis](https://www.studiomatrx.org/students/interior-design-thesis) (B) and common practice (K):
- An independent, research-driven project on a researchable issue, steered by a guide; **judged on reasoning more than renders.**
- Units: framing the topic → research and parameters (literature, case studies, site and user study, citation) → design development and detailing → presentation, report and viva.
- Final assessment usually: sheets + report + model(s) + viva with external examiner, internal guide and HoD.
- "A resolved smaller thesis beats an unresolved large one." Scale scope to what can be resolved; she can fully detail selected zones (e.g. Sun Gallery, Spectrum Chamber, star chamber, dome) instead of every room.
- Academic integrity: original work, honest citations, no invented data. Every fact on a sheet should trace to a source in `sources/SOURCES.md`.

## 3. Usual review sequence in Indian design colleges (K; confirm her actual dates)

1. Topic / synopsis (done, 26 Nov 2026)
2. Literature study + case studies (literature and live)
3. Site analysis and site selection justification
4. Area programme, zoning, concept development
5. Material selection / material board
6. Design development: plans, sections, RCP, elevations
7. Detailing: furniture, exhibit, ceiling, lighting, services
8. Pre-final: full set, model progress, report draft
9. Final jury / viva with external examiner: sheets, model(s), report, PPT/walkthrough

See `02_roadmap/review-roadmap.md` for what to deliver at each.
