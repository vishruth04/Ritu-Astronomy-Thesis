# Materials and finishes (for the material-selection review)

Status: Draft, 27 Nov 2026. Tags: A primary, B secondary, K general practice (verify suppliers and grades locally).

## 1. Selection criteria for this project

1. **Light behaviour** (the concept): reflectance from bright white/warm to matte black; transparency, refraction and colour from glass.
2. **Kodaikanal climate:** high humidity (87-89% Sep-Nov) and cool temperatures → moisture-resistant boards, no swelling, anti-mould finishes, warm-to-touch surfaces.
3. **Fire:** NBC Part 4 cl. 3.3.4 requires non-combustible false ceilings; exit routes need low flame-spread finishes.
4. **Durability** for school groups: impact-resistant, cleanable.
5. **Local and low-carbon** where possible: Palani Hills stone, local timber (with legal sourcing), lime.

## 3. Key materials explained

**Dichroic glass** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dichroic_glass), [Architizer](https://architizer.com/blog/inspiration/collections/dichroic-glass/amp/), B): many ultra-thin metal-oxide layers (30-50 layers, under 1 micron total) deposited in a vacuum. It transmits some colours and reflects their complements, and the colour shifts with viewing angle. Uses: Ohio State chiller plant fins (coloured shadows as the sun moves), Harpa Reykjavik (Schott Narima), museum facades. Cheaper alternative: **dichroic film** on clear glass or acrylic (3M). Perfect for the Spectrum Chamber: one material that literally splits light.

**Fibre-optic star ceilings** ([Schott](https://www.schott.com/en-gb/products/star-ceiling/product-variants), A): bundles of fibres all lit from one remote light source; one harness carries up to about 30 light points; modes include brighter "constellation" stars, faint "background" stars and "flickering" stars. Advantages: no heat or electricity at the ceiling, one serviceable light box. Local fibre-optic star-ceiling kits are available from Indian lighting suppliers (K). Fit fibres through black acoustic panels so the ceiling also absorbs sound.

**Perforated aluminium dome panels:** let sound and air pass, reduce reflections (ESO: 58% reflectivity with paint). See `dome-theatre.md`.

**Acoustic panels** (dark zones): PET felt (recycled, mould-resistant, fire-rated grades), fabric-wrapped mineral wool, wood-wool boards. Prefer PET felt and mineral wool over open foam in humid climate.

**Boards and joinery** (from `vishruth04/plinthscapes-os/office/skills/interior-detailing-and-specs`): IS 303 MR-grade plywood for dry interior joinery; IS 710 marine/BWP where damp; avoid plain MDF in humid zones (use HDHMR or exterior-grade MDF); compact laminate for exhibit tops.

**Floors:** terrazzo (cast-in-situ or tile) allows brass/metal inlay for sun-calendar lines; local granite is durable and regional; carpet tiles in dark rooms absorb sound.

**Paints:** low-VOC; anti-fungal for walls in humid months; matte black with low light reflectance (< 5%) in dark rooms; avoid gloss.

**Glass:** toughened/laminated safety glass (IS 16231) near floors and in exhibit cases.
