**For Kodaikanal (K):** a **10-12 m dome with 60-90 seats** suits a small-town museum with group visits of one or two school buses, fits the 10 m height cap if partly sunk, and can run several short shows an hour.

## 2. Key design choices

| Choice | Options | Recommendation for this thesis |
|---|---|---|
| Seating | **Concentric** (seats in rings facing the centre, flat dome; good for live sky talks, flexible) vs **unidirectional** (all seats face one way, tilted dome; cinematic, more seats) | Concentric on a flat floor: suits teaching, telescope nights and her "event space" idea, and allows removable seats. Seiler notes flat domes make removable seating easier. |
| Tilt | 0° (horizontal) to 25°+ | 0-10°. Seiler: tilt adds roughly 10%+ to dome cost, and steep tilt with stairs and handrails loses a dark horizon. |
| Spring line (dome base) | As low as possible for immersion (Kasian) | Seat backs high and reclined; keep the base near eye level of reclined viewers |
| Screen | Perforated aluminium panels, seams Standard / Premium / Hidden; gain about 0.48-0.65 | Perforated aluminium: lets sound through and air through, reduces reflections (ESO, Seiler) |
| Projection | Optical star projector, digital fulldome, or hybrid | One or two edge-mounted digital projectors (light, cheap, hides equipment at the perimeter). Optional small optical star ball as a sculptural centrepiece. |
| Cove lighting | LED cove 75-300 mm inside the dome base (Seiler) | RGB cove for twilight sunsets before shows: a direct Chiaroscuro moment |

## 3. Plan and section checklist (K)

- **Light-locked entry and exit:** two doors or a baffled L-shaped corridor so no daylight enters during a show; separate exit route.
- **Behind-the-dome space:** 0.6-1 m gap between the screen and the outer shell for speakers, ducts, cove lights and maintenance catwalk (Kasian mentions catwalks and rotating ladders).
- **Speakers behind the screen;** subwoofer under the floor or at the perimeter.
- **Acoustics:** the dome focuses sound to the centre (echoes). The perforated screen plus absorptive lining behind it (mineral wool, black fabric) solves this. Aim for a short, dry reverberation time (about 0.5-0.8 s, K).
- **HVAC:** quiet, low-velocity supply from the perimeter behind the dome, return high. Sealed dark rooms need fresh air.
- **Control console** at the back or edge with a clear view of the whole dome (Kasian).
- **Seats:** high-backed recliners; wheelchair spaces on the flat level (at least 1 per 100 seats; see codes file); companion seats beside them.
- **Floor:** carpet or acoustic flooring, dark; low-level aisle lights.
- **Pre-show space:** a dim waiting room that helps eyes adapt (fits the Chiaroscuro sequence: this is the "twilight" zone).

## 4. Interior design opportunities (what she can draw)

- The dome's outer shell as a sculptural object in the dark chamber (like the Hayden Sphere inside the Rose Center's glass cube).
- Entry ramp spiralling down around the dome (descent into darkness).
- Seat design and upholstery colour (deep blue/black, matte).
- Cove-light sunset at the start of every show.
- A "telescope night" mode: seats removed or folded so the flat floor becomes the event space she listed.
