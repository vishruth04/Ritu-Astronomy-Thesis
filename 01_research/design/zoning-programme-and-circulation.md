# Zoning, area programme and circulation

Status: Draft working programme, 27 Nov 2026. Areas are **first estimates (K)** for her to adjust against the drawn 66 x 66 m plan. Check the final plan area against this table.

## 1. Zoning idea: a journey from light to dark

```
ARRIVAL (bright)          SUN (brightest)        TWILIGHT (filtered)         NIGHT (dark)            DEEP SPACE (darkest)
entry, tickets, shop  ->  Sun Gallery,       ->  Spectrum Chamber,      ->  Cosmic scale corridor -> Star chamber,
cafe, toilets             sundial court          Moon & planets gallery     (light lock)              Dome theatre
         ^                                                                                                 |
         |________________________ exit / return route via workshop + event space + cafe ___________________|
```

- **Public, bright zone** (entry, shop, cafe, toilets) stays outside the dark sequence so people can leave or rest.
- **Exhibition sequence** is one-way (prevents crowding in dark rooms), with a short-cut exit at the midpoint.
- **Learning zone** (workshop, event space) has its own entry for schools and evening events, so it works when galleries are closed.
- **Service zone:** projection/AV room, plant, stores, staff, loading, exhibit workshop.

## 3. Circulation rules (K)

- One clear loop; never make visitors double back through a dark room.
- **Light locks** between brightness zones: a turn, a baffle wall or two doors. Each lock is also a story beat (dusk, moonrise, nightfall).
- Bench every 30-40 m and in each dark room (eyes need time; older visitors especially).
- Corridors 2.4-3 m in galleries so school groups can pass each other; wider at exhibits that draw crowds.
- Wayfinding in the dark: lit handrails, floor strips, tactile changes at thresholds.
- Group flow: a 40-student bus split into 2-3 groups of 15-20 rotating between dome show, workshop and galleries; size rooms for 20 at once.
